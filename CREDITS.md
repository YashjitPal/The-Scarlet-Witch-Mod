## Built on

- [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/) and [Fabric API](https://github.com/FabricMC/fabric), on Fabric
- [NeoForge](https://neoforged.net/), on NeoForge
- [Mixin](https://github.com/SpongePowered/Mixin) and [MixinExtras](https://github.com/LlamaLad7/MixinExtras), which both loaders provide
- Built with [Fabric Loom](https://github.com/FabricMC/fabric-loom) and [ModDevGradle](https://github.com/neoforged/ModDevGradle), set up from the [MultiLoader Template](https://github.com/jaredlll08/MultiLoader-Template)

The mod bundles no other code.

## Art and sound

- Every texture is pixel art drawn by the mod's own generator (`tools/assetgen`) from a single palette. Nothing is taken from the films, the comics, other games or Minecraft's own textures.
- Every sound is one of Minecraft's own, layered and pitched in `sounds.json`. The mod ships no recordings.
- The costumes take after Arrzee's Multiverse skins, used as reference only.

## Inspiration

Wanda Maximoff, the Scarlet Witch, as she appears in Marvel Studios' *WandaVision* and *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*: her chaos magic and tiara, the Hex around Westview and its sitcom eras, and the Darkhold.
